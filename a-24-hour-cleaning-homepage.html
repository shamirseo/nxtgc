Common questions

Not seeing your question? Call us and we will help.

(205) 960-1717
How much does cleaning cost?
Pricing depends on the size and condition of the space, the type of service and how often you need it. Call us with a few details and we will give you a clear price before any work begins.
Do I need to be home during the cleaning?
No. Many clients arrange access in advance and we lock up when we finish. If you prefer to be there, that works too.
Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?
Yes. Our team arrives with the products and equipment needed for the job. If you prefer specific products, tell us when you book.
Can I set up recurring cleanings?
Yes. Choose weekly, bi-weekly or monthly service for your home, or daily and weekly service for your business. You can change the schedule as your needs change.
What areas do you serve?
We are based in Birmingham, Alabama and serve the surrounding area. Call us with your address to confirm we cover your location.
What if I am not happy with the result?
Tell us as soon as possible and we will work to make it right. Your feedback also helps us fine-tune your cleaning plan.
