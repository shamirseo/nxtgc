FAQ

Questions, answered

Cannot find what you need? Call or text us and we will help.

Do you serve Birmingham and Tuscaloosa, AL?

We serve Birmingham, Tuscaloosa and surrounding areas in Alabama. If you are nearby, call or text and we will confirm.

What types of projects do you take on?

We handle new builds, remodels, flips and investment properties. Services include flooring, interior painting, bathroom renovations, tile showers, vanities and trim work.

Can you handle my whole renovation?

Yes. Whether it is one room or a whole-home renovation, we can manage it from start to finish so you work with one team.

How do I get a quote?

Call or text (205) 960-1717. We will talk through your project and schedule a walkthrough.

How much does a renovation cost?

Cost depends on the size of the space, the materials and the scope of work. After a walkthrough we map out the scope and budget with you before any work begins, so there are no surprises.

How long does a renovation take?

Timelines depend on the project. A single room such as a bathroom takes less time than a whole-home renovation. We set out the timeline with you during planning, before work starts.

Should I plan my project before I am ready to build?

Yes. Planning ahead gives you time to think through design, materials and budget. You do not need to start construction right away.

Do you work on rentals and investment properties?

Yes. We help landlords and investors with turnovers, updates and pre-sale renovations so properties are ready faster.

If you could tackle one project next, which would it be?

Kitchen, primary bathroom, new flooring, a whole-home renovation or outdoor and storm repairs. Let us help you plan it.
